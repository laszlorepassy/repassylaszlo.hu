Evening maths 2026

Mathematics, year 9

We meet once a week, for three mathematics lessons in a row. That is not enough on its own, though: mathematics has to be taken out and worked on individually at least once more every week. We will read from Dialogues on Mathematics, and we will also talk about the uses of mathematics and its philosophical background, but the primary goal is a successful final exam, and this year lays its foundations.

What you need

  • A printed textbook is not compulsory, but it is a good idea to use one.
  • An A4 mathematics exercise book is compulsory. It is for notes written by hand, in pen, during the lesson, and for homework — nothing else.
  • You will not need a calculator this year.

Topics of the school year

1. Combinatorics and sets

7 September. Pages 6–8 of Matematika 9., volume I are the material to study; the homework is exercises 5 and 6 on page 8. Besides that, let us think over once more, based on our notes, why we study mathematics.

2. The world of numbers

3. Equations and identities

4. Introduction to geometry

5. Functions

6. Congruence, quadrilaterals

Requirements

  • Weekly homework: written by hand, solved in the exercise book, compulsory. Since we only meet once a week, this makes sure that mathematics comes up at least twice every week.
  • Tests: we write a test at least once a quarter.
  • How to pass the subject: anyone who pays attention in class, keeps their exercise book, does the homework and can solve the simpler standard exercises on their own is certain to succeed.
